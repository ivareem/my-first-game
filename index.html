How to play

  1. Choose one empty square. The board locks while you fight for it.
  2. Counter the alien move. A correct response hurts the alien; another response hurts you.
  3. Win the battle for X. Lose it for O. There is no extra alien board turn.
  4. Make an X line before the aliens make an O line.
